## Context paragraph

This project is a machine-vision system that estimates vital signs and stress level from synchronised RGB and thermal video of a person's face, without any contact sensor. It uses remote photoplethysmography (rPPG): each heartbeat causes a tiny, invisible change in skin colour that an RGB camera can measure, while a thermal (long-wave infrared) camera measures skin temperature. In the RGB stream, MediaPipe FaceLandmarker finds 478 3D face landmarks and the head pose (rotation and translation); the forehead and cheeks are split into about 25 small skin patches defined on the face mesh; the mean R, G, B of each patch is tracked over time; and the POS algorithm turns each patch's colour signal into a candidate pulse signal. In the thermal stream, the face landmarks are mapped onto the thermal frame (RGB-thermal registration); the temperature of each patch, the nose tip and the nostril region is measured; the nostril temperature oscillation gives a breathing signal and breathing rate; and the nose-tip temperature trend is tracked because the nose tip cools under stress. Every few seconds each patch receives a trust score from five checks: pulse quality (share of spectral power in the sharpest peak), agreement (weighted vote on the peak frequency across patches), face orientation (patch surface normal from the 3D landmarks), brightness (shadow or shine) and a thermal skin check (is the patch actually at skin temperature, which rejects hair, glasses and occlusion). The patch pulses are combined as a trust-weighted average with a confidence value; the part of the signal that follows head movement is removed using the head pose; the heart rate is read from the FFT peak while rejecting peaks at the breathing rate and its harmonics; beat detection gives heart-rate variability (RMSSD, SDNN, LF/HF). Finally a stress classifier uses heart rate, HRV, breathing rate and the nose-tip temperature trend, from confident windows only, to output a stress level (low, moderate or high). The system outputs heart rate, breathing rate, HRV, stress level and a confidence value, and reports "signal unreliable" when confidence is low. It needs no training of a deep network and runs on a laptop CPU.
